06 — Answers & Insights

Frequently asked.

Why expand to an omnichannel framework (Meta, Google, and TikTok)?

Relying on a single platform leaves your brand exposed to sudden algorithm fluctuations, high ad costs, and tracking drops. By capturing demand across Google, driving visual intent through Meta, and sourcing high-volume cultural interest on TikTok, we secure a diversified, stable stream of revenue.

How do you handle ad creatives between platforms?

We don't reuse assets lazily. What converts on a Google Shopping feed will fail entirely on TikTok. We build distinct, platform-native ad positioning guidelines—clean copy and strong product angles for search intent, polished lifestyle layouts for Meta feeds, and raw, high-velocity hooks for TikTok loops.

Why don't you share live dashboard access?

We protect our clients' data and competitive advantages fiercely. Publicly publishing actual multi-channel product insights opens the door for competitors to copy winning assets. We keep operations clean and confidential, walk you through strategies on live screen-shares, and send plain-English summaries directly to you.
